# Tallystead Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Tallystead contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and differing perspectives offered in good faith.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private, financial, employment, or identifying information.
- Use fictional or sanitized data when discussing defects and examples.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal insults, sustained disruption, trolling, or bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Pressuring anyone to disclose household, financial, security, or identity information.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in Tallystead repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the contact information published by the Tallystead GitHub organization. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. If no private contact method is available, open a neutral issue requesting private maintainer contact without naming people or describing the incident.

Maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical. They may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban participation depending on severity and pattern. Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report.

Knowingly false or retaliatory reports may themselves violate this code. A report that cannot be substantiated is not, by itself, a false report.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for applying these standards consistently and may reject contributions or participation that undermine project safety, privacy, or constructive collaboration.
